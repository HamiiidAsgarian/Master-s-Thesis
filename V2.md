# Project Roadmap & Evolution Plan (V2)

This document outlines the planned upgrades, fixes, and research expansions for the Off-Grid Energy Sharing MARL simulation.

## 1. Immediate Fixes & Ref_inements (Short-Term)

- [ ] **Logging Granularity**: Implement a more detailed `log_history` to capture per--episode energy flows between specific house pairs (to visualize "who helps whom").
- [ ] **Dynamic Weather**: Replace the binary "Cloudy/Clear" 15% probability with a Markov Chain weather model (e.g., transitions from Sunny -> Cloudy -> Rainy).
- [ ] **Reward Normalization**: Ensure `community_fairness` and `own_comfort` are scaled identically to prevent one weight from dominating the gradient during learning.

## 2. Model Fidelity Upgrades (Medium-Term)

### A. Physical Environment Enhancements

- [ ] **Battery Degradation**: Introduce a "Cycle Count" for batteries; frequent deep discharging reduces capacity over time, affecting long-term agent strategy.
- [ ] **Transmission Losses**: Add a small percentage loss (e.g., 2-5%) for energy shared between neighbors to simulate real-world wiring.
- [ ] **Inverter Efficiency**: Model the conversion loss from DC (Solar/Battery) to AC (House Demand).

### B. Network Topology Expansion

- [ ] **Mesh/Star Topedopies**: Move beyond the Ring topology. Test how "Hub" houses (centralized) vs. "Peer-to-Peer" networks affect fairness.
- [ ] **Dynamic Neighbors**: Allow agents to "choose" neighbors based on historical reliability or proximity.

## 3. Algorithmic & Agent Upgrades (Research Expansion)

### A. From Tabular to Deep RL

- [ ] **DQN / PPO Implementation**: Replace `QLAgent` with a Deep Q-Network (DQN) or Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO). This allows for continuous state spaces (exact battery % and exact solar output).
- [ ] **Multi-Agent Actor-Critic (MAPPO)**: Explore centralized training with decentralized execution to see if global coordination improves fairness.

### B. Personality & Social Dynamics

- [ ] **Evolving Personalities**: Instead of fixed weights, allow personalities to "drift" based on experience (e.g., an agent becomes more selfish if it experiences repeated blackouts).
- [ ] **Reputation System**: Implement a7 Trust Score." Agents share more with neighbors who have high trust scores.

## 4. Economic & Policy Analysis (Thesis Depth)

- [ ] **Dynamic Pricing Model**: Introduce a "Community Tariff" where agents can trade energy at fluctuatingTORating prices based on scarcity.
- [ed] Governance Scenarios: Simulate different "Rules of Engagement":
  - _Mandatory Sharing_: A fixed % of surplus must be shared.
  - _Incentivzed Sharing_: Prosocial agents receive a "community credit."
- [ ] **Sensitivity Analysis Expansion**: Run simulations across varying population sizes (6 to 50 houses) to test scalability.

## 5. Visualization & UI

- [ ] **Real-time Dashboard**: A Streamlit or Dash interface to visualize the energy flow and battery levels in real-time during a simulation run.
- [ ] **Heatmap Evolution**: Generate a video/GIF of Q-table heatmaps changing over training episodes.

## 6. Data & Statistics

- [ ] **Bootstrapping**: Increase seed count for final thesis results to ensure statistical significance (p < 0.01).
- [ ] **Correlation Matrix**: Analyze the correlation between "Initial Personality" and "Final Community Stability."
