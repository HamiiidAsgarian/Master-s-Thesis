The following guide is synthesized from general Azad University regulations and standard academic practices for Computer Science theses. **It is crucial that you confirm all specific requirements with your supervisor, as they have the final authority.**

### 📚 Overall Thesis Structure & Page Distribution

A Master's thesis at Azad University typically consists of five chapters. While the university does not specify a fixed number of pages, emphasizing quality over quantity, there is a generally observed range. The table below provides a practical guide based on a typical thesis of **100 to 150 pages**. More data-heavy research, common in Computer Science, can extend up to 200 pages with appendices.

| Chapter | Title                                                    | Typical Pages | Approx. Words\* |
| :------ | :------------------------------------------------------- | :------------ | :-------------- |
| **1**   | Introduction and Generalities (کلیات تحقیق)              | 10 – 20       | 3,000 – 6,000   |
| **2**   | Literature Review and Background (ادبیات و پیشینه تحقیق) | 25 – 40       | 7,500 – 12,000  |
| **3**   | Research Methodology (روش‌شناسی تحقیق)                   | 15 – 25       | 4,500 – 7,500   |
| **4**   | Data Analysis and Findings (تجزیه و تحلیل داده‌ها)       | 20 – 35       | 6,000 – 10,500  |
| **5**   | Conclusion and Suggestions (نتیجه‌گیری و پیشنهادات)      | 10 – 15       | 3,000 – 4,500   |

_Note: Word counts are approximate and based on standard Persian formatting. Your total thesis (Chapters 1-5) should ideally be between **70 to 150 pages**._

### 📝 Detailed Breakdown of Each Chapter

#### Chapter 1: Introduction and Generalities (کلیات تحقیق)

This is the "shop window" of your research and must be compelling.

- **What to Include**:
  1.  **Introduction (مقدمه)**: A broad overview of your topic to engage the reader and set the context.
  2.  **Problem Statement (بیان مسئله)**: Clearly and precisely define the research problem or gap your thesis will address.
  3.  **Significance and Necessity of the Research (اهمیت و ضرورت تحقیق)**: Explain why your research is important. What scientific or practical problems will it solve?
  4.  **Research Objectives (اهداف تحقیق)**: State the main goal and specific objectives you aim to achieve.
  5.  **Research Questions (سوالات تحقیق)**: List the primary and secondary questions your research will answer.
  6.  **Research Hypotheses (فرضیه‌های تحقیق)**: (If applicable) State your testable predictions.
  7.  **Research Methodology (روش تحقیق)**: Briefly introduce the methods you will use (e.g., simulation, algorithm design, data analysis).
  8.  **Scope and Limitations (قلمرو و محدودیت‌های تحقیق)**: Define the boundaries of your study (e.g., time, place, subject matter).
  9.  **Definition of Key Terms (تعریف مفاهیم و اصطلاحات)**: Define specialized terms used in your thesis.
- **What to Avoid**: Overly long historical background; this belongs in Chapter 2. Don't present results or conclusions.

---

#### Chapter 2: Literature Review and Background (ادبیات و پیشینه تحقیق)

This chapter provides the theoretical foundation for your work.

- **What to Include**:
  1.  **Theoretical Foundations (مبانی نظری)**: A comprehensive review of the theories, concepts, and models relevant to your topic.
  2.  **Review of Related Literature (پیشینه تحقیق)**: A critical review of previous studies, both Iranian and international. Organize this chronologically or thematically, and highlight the research gap your work will fill.
  3.  **Conceptual Framework (چارچوب conceptual یا مدل تحقیق)**: (If applicable) Present a model or framework that guides your research based on the literature.
- **What to Avoid**: Presenting a list of summaries without critical analysis. Avoid including information that is not directly relevant to your research problem.

---

#### Chapter 3: Research Methodology (روش‌شناسی تحقیق)

This chapter explains the "how" of your research, allowing others to replicate it.

- **What to Include**:
  1.  **Research Design and Approach (طرح و روش تحقیق)**: Describe your research type (e.g., applied, developmental) and approach (e.g., quantitative, qualitative, mixed).
  2.  **Data Collection Methods (روش‌های گردآوری داده‌ها)**: Explain your sources. For Computer Science, this could be datasets, simulation logs, or survey instruments.
  3.  **Tools and Instruments (ابزارهای تحقیق)**: List the software, libraries, hardware, or questionnaires used (e.g., Python with Scikit-learn, NS-3 simulator, specific APIs).
  4.  **Population, Sample, and Sampling Method (جامعه، نمونه و روش نمونه‌گیری)**: (If applicable) Define your target population and how the sample was selected.
  5.  **Data Analysis Methods (روش‌های تجزیه و تحلیل داده‌ها)**: Describe the statistical or analytical techniques you will use (e.g., regression analysis, algorithm comparison, performance evaluation metrics).
- **What to Avoid**: Discussing results or findings. This chapter should be a clear, step-by-step "recipe" for your research.

---

#### Chapter 4: Data Analysis and Findings (تجزیه و تحلیل داده‌ها)

This is the core of your research where you present your results.

- **What to Include**:
  1.  **Descriptive Analysis (توصیف و تحلیل داده‌ها)**: (If applicable) Present general characteristics of your data.
  2.  **Testing Hypotheses / Presenting Findings (آزمون فرضیه‌ها / یافته‌های تحقیق)**: Present the results of your experiments, simulations, or data analysis clearly and logically.
  3.  **Data Visualization**: Use tables, charts, and graphs to effectively communicate your findings.
  4.  **Interpretation and Analysis**: Provide an objective explanation of what the results mean without yet drawing final conclusions.
- **What to Avoid**: Raw, unprocessed data. Do not interpret your results in a way that goes beyond what the data shows.

---

#### Chapter 5: Conclusion and Suggestions (نتیجه‌گیری و پیشنهادات)

This chapter summarizes your work and looks to the future.

- **What to Include**:
  1.  **Summary of Findings (خلاصه یافته‌ها)**: Briefly summarize the most important results from Chapter 4.
  2.  **Conclusion (نتیجه‌گیری)**: Answer your main research question(s) and confirm or reject your hypotheses based on the findings.
  3.  **Research Contributions (دستاوردهای تحقیق)**: Explicitly state the contribution your thesis makes to the field.
  4.  **Research Limitations (محدودیت‌های تحقیق)**: Acknowledge any limitations encountered during the research that might affect the results.
  5.  **Suggestions for Future Research (پیشنهادات برای پژوهش‌های آتی)**: Propose specific directions for future studies based on your findings and the limitations of your work.
  6.  **Practical Suggestions (پیشنهادات کاربردی)**: (If applicable) Offer recommendations for how your findings can be applied in industry or practice.
- **What to Avoid**: Introducing new data or arguments not previously discussed.

### 💎 Final Recommendation

2.  **Check the Official Portal**: Visit the official "Research and Technology" page of Ardabil Branch (`ardabil.iau.ir`) for any posted regulations or forms. The "اداره امور پایان نامه" (Thesis Affairs Office) is a key resource.
3.  **Use the "Pazhooheshyar" System**: Register your thesis topic and proposals through the official **سامانه پژوهشیار** (Pazhooheshyar) system, as this is the standard platform for thesis management across Azad University.
