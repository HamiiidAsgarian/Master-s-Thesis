### **Justification for Specific Metric Selections**

- **Robustness via Blackout Rate:** You operationalized robustness using the Blackout Day Rate because it captures the system's "failure floor" during external weather shocks (cloudy days), which you found was more influential than agent personality at the community level.
- **Fairness Validation via Gini:** While Jain's Index is your primary fairness metric due to its scale-invariance, the Gini Coefficient was essential for Sub-question 4, as it was the most sensitive metric for detecting the "social noise" and inequality caused by the Innovator agent.
- **Efficiency via Energy Waste:** Traditional MARL energy papers (like May & Huang, 2023) use "community net loss". You instead used Energy Waste (kWh) to provide a more direct physical measure of how personality-driven sharing reduces technical inefficiency.
