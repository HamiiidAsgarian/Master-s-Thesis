### **Key Qualitative Positioning and INNOVATION**

1.  **Bridging the Technical and Psychological:** While technical papers like **Charbonnier et al.** and **May & Huang** provide the grid physics, and social simulations like **Ren & Xu** provide the behavioral grounding, your thesis is the first to couple **OCEAN personality coordinates** directly with **physical energy sharing actions** (e.g., mapping Agreeableness to sharing weights $w_{fair}$).
2.  **Addressing the Homogeneity Gap:** Most previous works treat agents as homogeneous or assign arbitrary "preferences". Your work provides a **theoretical "why"** for agent behavior based on established Five-Factor Theory.
3.  **The Discovery of the "Innovator Effect":** Your thesis goes beyond the pros vs. cons of existing literature by identifying a new phenomenon—how individual **Openness (Exploration)** functions as "social noise," destabilizing community coordination norms even when rewards are ostensibly fair.
4.  **Efficiency-Fairness Synergy:** Unlike SOTA that often sees fairness as a "tax" on efficiency, your qualitative analysis proves that prosocial behavior (sharing surplus) is **Pareto-improving**, reducing system waste by 11.5% while increasing individual comfort.
