# Islamic Azad University — Master of Computer Science Thesis Style Cheat Sheet

> **⚠️ Important Note:** This cheat sheet is based on publicly available guidelines for Islamic Azad University (IAU). However, **formatting requirements may vary by branch** (e.g., Science & Research Branch, Tehran Central, Dubai, etc.). Always confirm with your specific branch's postgraduate office and your supervisor before final submission.

## 1. Document Structure (Order of Sections)

Your thesis must follow this exact order:

| Order | Section                                      |
| ----- | -------------------------------------------- |
| 1     | Persian Title Page (صفحه عنوان فارسی)        |
| 2     | Approval Page (صفحه تصویب نامه)              |
| 3     | Dedication Page (صفحه تقدیم نامه) — optional |
| 4     | Acknowledgments (صفحه سپاسگزاری)             |
| 5     | Table of Contents (فهرست مطالب)              |
| 6     | List of Tables (فهرست جداول)                 |
| 7     | List of Figures/Diagrams (فهرست نمودارها)    |
| 8     | Persian Abstract (چکیده فارسی)               |
| 9     | Introduction (مقدمه)                         |
| 10    | Main Chapters (فصل‌های اصلی)                 |
| 11    | Conclusion (نتیجه‌گیری)                      |
| 12    | References (منابع و مراجع)                   |
| 13    | Appendices (پیوست‌ها)                        |
| 14    | English Abstract (چکیده انگلیسی)             |

## 2. Page Setup (Word Layout)

| Setting                    | Requirement                                     |
| -------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |

## 4. Title Page (صفحه عنوان)

The title page is the **first official page** of the thesis. IAU is very strict about its format.

**Required elements (all centered / وسط چین):**

1. University name: "Islamic Azad University"
2. Branch name
3. Thesis title
4. Student name
5. Supervisor name
6. Advisor name (if applicable)
7. Major and field of study
8. Defense date

**⚠️ No images, logos, or extra design elements on the title page.**

## 5. Cover Page (جلد) — Physical Binding

### 5.1 Front Cover Layout

| Element             | Specification                                                              |
| ------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **University Logo** | 4 cm × 3 cm, positioned in top-left corner, 1.5 cm from top and left edges |
| **All text**        | Centered horizontally                                                      |
| **Spine (عطف)**     | Thesis title and author name in gold/silver embossing                      |
| **Multi-volume**    | Volume number at bottom of spine                                           |

### 5.2 Front Cover Text Order (top to bottom):

1. University logo (top-left)
2. "Islamic Azad University" — Zarr 18 Bold
3. Branch name — Zarr 18 Bold
4. Faculty and Department — Zarr 16 Bold
5. "Thesis for Master of Science Degree (M.Sc.)" — Zarr 16 Bold
   - Use **M.Sc.** for non-humanities (including Computer Science)
   - Use **M.A.** for humanities
6. Field/Major — Zarr 16 Bold
7. "Title" — Nazanin 18 Bold
8. Thesis Title — Titr 18 Bold
9. "Supervisor" — Nazanin 18 Bold
10. Supervisor Name — Zarr 18 Bold
11. "Advisor(s)" — Nazanin 18 Bold
12. Advisor Name(s) — Zarr 18 Bold
13. "Author" — Nazanin 18 Bold
14. Author Name — Zarr 18 Bold
15. Defense Date (Year & Season) — Zarr 14 Bold

## 6. Front Matter Pages (صفحات فرعی / ابتدایی)

| Section                           | Specification                                                                                                           |
| --------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **First page after cover**        | "Bismillah al-Rahman al-Rahim" (بسم الله الرحمن الرحیم)                                                                 |
| **Page numbering (Front Matter)** | Persian alphabet letters (الف، ب، پ...) or Abjad numerals for Persian theses                                            |
| **Page numbering (Body)**         | Arabic numerals (1, 2, 3...) starting from the main text                                                                |
| **Approval Page**                 | Completed after defense; includes: thesis title, student name, supervisor, examiners, defense date, official signatures |
| **Dedication**                    | Optional; brief, formal text                                                                                            |
| **Acknowledgments**               | Brief paragraph thanking supervisor, advisors, examiners, and family                                                    |

## 7. Persian Abstract (چکیده فارسی)

| Specification | Requirement                                                          |
| ------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Length**    | 150–250 words                                                        |
| **Content**   | Most important section of the thesis; must summarize the entire work |

## 8. Chapter & Heading Numbering

### 8.1 Chapter Structure

| Level          | Format                  | Example |
| -------------- | ----------------------- | ------- |
| **Chapter**    | Persian numeral + "فصل" | فصل اول |
| **Section**    | Decimal numbering       | ۱-۱     |
| **Subsection** | Decimal numbering       | ۱-۱-۱   |

### 8.2 Heading Styles in Word

| Style         | Suggested Use                               |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| **Heading 1** | Chapter titles — Bold, larger font          |
| **Heading 2** | Section titles — Bold                       |
| **Heading 3** | Subsection titles — Bold, italic (optional) |

Use Word's **built-in heading styles** to enable automatic Table of Contents generation.

## 9. Lists & Tables

| **List of Figures**   | Auto-generated from Word's figure captions |

## 14. Important Reminders

1. **Branch-specific variations:** Each IAU branch may have slight differences in fonts, sizes, or numbering. Always check your branch's specific style manual.
2. **Approval page:** Must be completed after defense with official signatures — do not modify its format.
3. **Computer Science:** Use **M.Sc.** (not M.A.) on the cover.
4. **Language:** If your thesis is in English, the cover must still have Persian text on the front, with English translation on the back cover.
5. **Template:** Consider downloading a pre-formatted Word template for your specific IAU branch to save time.
