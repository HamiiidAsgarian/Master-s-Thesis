## 3. NUMBERING SYSTEM

Sections are numbered using a **hyphen-based system** (not decimal dots):

| Level           | Format                       | Example | Meaning                             |
| :-------------- | :--------------------------- | :------ | :---------------------------------- |
| **Chapter**     | Single number                | `2`     | Chapter 2                           |
| **Section**     | `Chapter-Section`            | `2-4`   | Chapter 2, Section 4                |
| **Sub-section** | `Chapter-Section-Subsection` | `2-4-3` | Chapter 2, Section 4, Sub-section 3 |
| **Equations**   | `Chapter-Number`             | `6-8`   | 8th equation in Chapter 6           |

> **Right-to-left reading**: In the Persian document, the chapter number is on the **right**, and the section number is on the **left** (e.g., `2-4` means Chapter 2, Section 4).

## 4. CITATION & REFERENCE STYLE

### In-Text Citations (Author-Date Method)

> **Rule**: In the thesis text, instead of a number and reference row, the **first and last name** and the **year of publication** should be mentioned.

**Example**: (مردوخی، احمد، ۱۳۷۵)

### References Section

- **Farsi sources** listed first, sorted alphabetically
- **Non-Farsi (English, etc.)** sources listed second, sorted alphabetically
- Follow **APA** or **Vancouver** style as prescribed by your faculty

### Latin/Specialized Terms

- Avoid using English/Latin words in the Farsi text
- Use **footnotes** to provide the original Latin spelling for specialized terms

## 5. CHAPTER-SPECIFIC CONTENT REQUIREMENTS

| Chapter                  | Must Include                                                                                  | Must NOT Include                            |
| :----------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------ |
| **1: Introduction**      | Problem statement, Significance, Objectives, Hypotheses, Definitions                          | Detailed literature review, Data, Results   |
| **2: Literature Review** | Theoretical framework, Previous studies (domestic & int'l), Conceptual models                 | New research, Methodology details, Results  |
| **3: Methodology**       | Research design, Population, Sample, Tools (validity/reliability), Procedure, Analysis method | Results, Discussion, Literature review      |
| **4: Data Analysis**     | Objective presentation of findings, Tables, Charts, Statistical outputs                       | Interpretation, Discussion, Recommendations |
| **5: Discussion**        | Interpretation, Comparison with previous studies, Limitations, Future suggestions             | New data, New literature (only refer back)  |
